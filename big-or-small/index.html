Calm by design

Built on the way children learn concepts: hear an instruction, tap a picture, get gentle feedback, and a hint after a miss.

  • Only the pictures on screen. Tap empty space to hear the question again.
  • Every question is spoken in the best voice on the device, at a slow, relaxed or normal pace. A parent or grandparent can even record a Personal Voice so questions come in a familiar voice.
  • No countdown. Speed is measured but never required.
  • No streak to lose. Missing a day costs nothing.
  • Sparkles, coins and confetti for right answers, and Reduce Motion turns them off.
  • Large text and tap targets, light and dark mode, and VoiceOver labels on everything.

Questions

I already have Big or Small 2. What happens?

Big or Small 7 arrives as a free update to the same app. The first time it opens, it brings across your recent answers (as Player 1, which you can rename) and your settings, on your device. Badges are earned again from that history.

Who is it for?

Young children first, including children with language delays and autistic children who like a calm, predictable routine. Older children and adults can keep sharp, and older adults can play alongside family.

Is it a diagnostic test?

No. Big or Small shows how a player is doing in its own games, compared only with their own earlier play. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it doesn't replace advice from a doctor, health visitor or teacher.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Players, answers and reports stay in the app's storage on your device and in your device's own backups. There's no account, no analytics and no internet needed. See the privacy policy.

Can little hands change the settings?

Reports, settings and the reward goal sit behind a simple grown-ups' check.
