Look, remember, find it.

SeenQuest is I Saw It, rebuilt. Look at the pictures, tap Ready, then find the one you saw. You can understand it in seconds and play for a minute or an hour, and there's no timer unless you want one.

I Saw It Classic

The board you answer on is the one to remember next, just as it always was.

  • Five lives, and boards that grow from 2×2 to 5×5 as your score climbs.
  • Standard is the original. Relaxed pauses after every answer, and misses cost no points.
  • Challenge adds a few seconds per board, and pictures that look alike.

Memory Journey

100 levels in five worlds. No lives, no energy and no waiting. Earn up to three stars on each.

  • Memory Garden: find the pictures you saw.
  • Hidden Forest: what's missing, and which square changed?
  • Pattern Valley: where was it, and in what order?
  • Mystery Island: a bit of everything.
  • Memory Castle: swaps, colour changes and long sequences.

A puzzle every day

The Daily Challenge is the same five rounds for everyone on the same date. The first play counts toward your streak, and you can share your result without giving the answers away.

  • Your Progress shows your Classic best, levels, stars, accuracy, streak and achievements.
  • Game results only. SeenQuest is a game. It doesn't measure, prevent or treat memory loss or any other condition.

Questions

I played I Saw It. What changed?

SeenQuest is I Saw It rebuilt from scratch, as an update to the same app. The original game is here as I Saw It Classic, with Relaxed and Challenge styles, alongside the Journey, the Daily Challenge and two new modes. Everyone starts with a fresh score.

Does SeenQuest need an internet connection?

No. Every level and every Daily Challenge is made on your device. A connection is used only to buy or restore Premium.

Is there an account, or ads?

Neither. There's no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.

Is SeenQuest brain training?

No. It's a game, and its statistics are game results. It isn't a medical product.

How do I restore Premium?

Open Settings, then Premium, and tap Restore Purchases. Premium is shared with your Family Sharing group.
