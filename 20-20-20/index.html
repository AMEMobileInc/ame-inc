20.20.20Eye care & progress

Look far.
Rest your eyes.

20.20.20 helps you build one simple habit: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Gentle reminders and a calm timer make it easier to keep going.

View on the App Store ↗

For iPhone and Apple Watch. Free to start, with optional 20.20.20 Plus.

One habit, three numbers

The 20-20-20 rule turns a long screen day into a rhythm of small, useful pauses.

20

Minutes

Choose a reminder rhythm that fits your day: 20, 30, 40, or 60 minutes.

20

Feet

Look into the distance and let your eyes change focus away from the screen.

20

Seconds

A calm timer, with haptics and a gentle finish, helps you complete the break.

A break that follows you

Start where you are. The iPhone and Apple Watch work together, or independently when that is more convenient.

iPhone reminders

Set your days, hours, interval, haptics, and sound. A notification takes you straight into the break.

Apple Watch support

Run the timer on your wrist, with haptics and a quick way to pause or finish.

Make the habit visible

Progress should feel encouraging, not demanding. 20.20.20 keeps the useful details close at hand.

Flexible reminders

Work around your schedule with chosen days, working hours, and intervals.

Gentle momentum

Track today, your streak, and your recent history without turning rest into a competition.

Private by design

No account is required. Your routine and progress stay on your devices.

Free for the habit. Plus for more.

Breaks and reminders stay free for everyone. 20.20.20 Plus adds custom intervals, more pause options, history and trends, achievements, alternate icons, ring themes, and a Supporter badge.
