Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your list and recipes stay on your iPhone. New recipes download from our server, which receives nothing about you beyond the request itself.

Are the recipes healthy?

They're vegetable-forward, quick, and labelled honestly: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, high in protein or fibre. They're not medical or nutrition advice.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The starter recipes and everything you've downloaded work without a connection; new recipes arrive when you're back online.
