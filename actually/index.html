Photos, voice, video

Made for children who communicate in their own way, including children with autism, speech delays or other communication differences, and for the families, teachers and therapists who support them.

1

Pick photos

Choose photos from your library in the order you want them. Actually keeps its own copy, so your stories work offline.

2

Tell the story

Tap the big red button and say what happened in each photo. Add a few words under any photo if you like.

3

Share the video

Play the story full screen, then make a video right on your device to save to Photos or share with family, school or therapy.

A closer look

Large buttons, one photo at a time, and nothing on the main path that needs reading or typing.

For every kind of voice

Not every child can record, and that's fine.

No recording? Words are read aloud

Type a few words under a photo and the device reads them when the story plays, and in the video. Choose the reading voice and how long each photo stays on screen.

Made to be easy to use

Big, clear buttons, VoiceOver labels and larger text support. Photos can be put in order with Move Up and Move Down buttons, not only by dragging.

Grown-ups stay in charge

Settings, purchases and links sit behind a quick sum for grown-ups, so little fingers stay in the story.

Questions

I used Message Actually. What happens to my stories?

When you update to Actually, your stories, photos and recordings are brought across on your device the first time it opens. Message Actually's own files aren't changed. Your Actually Plus is free for life.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. There's no account, and stories, playing and videos all work offline. A connection is only needed to buy Plus or to share a video.

Where are my stories kept?

On your iPhone or iPad and in its backups. Videos are made on your device. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it. There are no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Is this a therapy or medical app?

No. Actually is a storytelling tool. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition, and it works well alongside the support a child already has.
