Coloring CityPaint the Locks, Find the Exit

Paint every lock. Find the exit.

A bright pixel platformer made by Jaey, a young game maker, and rebuilt from the ground up. Grab a paint jar to change colour, touch every lock in its colour, and run for the exit before the timer runs out.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone, iPad and Mac. All 40 stages free, with an optional Plus. No account, no ads, no tracking. Plays offline. Coloring City 2 arrives as a free update.

How to play

Every stage is a small puzzle you run through.

  1. Take a colour

    Touch a paint jar and your painter takes its colour.

  2. Paint the locks

    Touch every lock in its own colour. Some need colours you don't have yet.

  3. Beat the clock

    Coins add time: silver one second, gold five, diamonds thirty. Collect the three stars if you can.

  4. Reach the exit

    When the last lock is painted, the exit opens. Fewer hits and more time left make a better score.

Mix your colours

Jaey's colour rules, the way paint and light really mix.

  • Black plus mixes like ink: cyan and magenta make blue, and all three make black.
  • White plus mixes like light: red and green make yellow, and all three make white.
  • Minus takes a colour back out, so the order you pick things up matters.
  • Switches, keys, portals and jump pads join in as the areas go on, with drones, spikes and floor guards in the way.

Questions

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or later, including the iPhone Duo, and Mac. Plus bought on one unlocks on all of them with the same Apple Account.

I played Coloring City before. What happens to my progress?

Your stars and best scores carry over to version 2, and you get Plus free, for good. Settings shows "Original player". The game checks once with the App Store, so it needs a connection the first time.

Does it need an internet connection, or an account?

Neither. Everything plays offline. Game Center is optional and used only for leaderboards; a connection is used only to buy or restore Plus.

Who made it?

Jaey made the game, and Jaey's brother made its music, "End of Drought". AME Mobile rebuilt it for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

I bought Plus on another device. How do I get it back?

Open Plus from the title screen or Settings and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account.
