Sleepy PiFall Asleep to π

The most boring bedtime story ever told.

Sleepy Pi reads you the digits of π, one per heartbeat, softly, in your language, over the sound of a real shore. There's no pattern to follow and nothing to remember, so your mind lets go.

Download on the App Store ↗

For iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Free, with optional Sleepy Pi Plus. No account, no ads, no tracking.

How a night goes

Choose how far to go, lie back, and let the digits drift past.

314

Pick a distance

314 digits is about ten minutes. Go to 3141 or 31415 on longer nights.

3.14

It slows with you

The pace slows, the screen dims and the voice fades as you near the end.

zZ

It notices you're asleep

When the room has been dark and still for a while, Sleepy Pi stops and remembers the night.

Spoken softly, or played as notes

The digits are read by the voices built into your iPhone, hushed and slowed, like someone reading in the next room.

  • Voice, Notes or both: each digit can be a note from a music box or a piano. The tune of π.
  • Soft, Distant or Natural: pick how close the voice sounds.
  • Your language: your device's language and English are free; more than thirty with Plus.

The sound of a real shore

Beach videos from Cape Breton and the Bay of Fundy are the room's white noise.

  • Seen or just heard: let the picture glow dimly behind the digits, or hide it and listen to the waves.
  • No seams: each pass blends into the next, so the sea never stops.
  • Offline: videos are kept on your device after the first night. Plain white noise is made on the device and never loops.

The boring world of π

For the daytime, too.

  • Find your birthday in the first million digits.
  • Look up any number, like 2718 or 0000.
  • A little π trivia each night.

Your nights

See how many minutes it took to fall asleep, how far into π you got, your streak and your farthest night.

Free, or Plus?

Everything you need for a night is free. Sleepy Pi Plus opens the rest of the collection: $0.99 a month, $9.99 a year, or once for life. If you bought Sleepy Pi back when it was a paid app, Plus is already yours.

Longer nights

The marathon and the endless night: 314159 digits, or all one million. Plus charts of your nights, with export.

Questions

I bought Sleepy Pi years ago. Do I pay again?

No. Update the app and Plus is already unlocked as an Original Supporter. If it isn't, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only for the first night with each beach video, and to buy or restore Plus. After that everything works offline.

Is there an account, or ads?

Neither. No sign-in, no advertising and no tracking. Your nights stay on your device.

Is Sleepy Pi a medical product?

No. It's a calm, boring way to wind down, not a treatment for insomnia or any other condition.
