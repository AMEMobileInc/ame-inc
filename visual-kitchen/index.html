Shop by picture

A list that works whether you read it or recognise it.

  • Every item gets a picture as you add it: lemons, milk, pasta, eggs.
  • Your own photos: hold an item to photograph the brand you buy. It's used on every list after that.
  • Tap to hear it. Names can be read aloud as you check things off.
  • Sorted by aisle, with amounts added up across recipes, and "Buy again" for the weekly things.

Print a picture list

For practising at the shop, life-skills lessons, or the fridge door.

  • Your shopping list, or a recipe's ingredients and tools, as a page of pictures with names and amounts.
  • Your photos print too, so the page matches the shelf.
  • Print it, save it as a PDF, or send it.

Cook step by step

Each step shows what it uses.

  • Pictures of the ingredients and tools for each step, in large type, with the screen kept on.
  • A timer for each step, with a notification if your phone is locked.
  • Steps read aloud, so messy hands stay off the screen.
  • "You'll need" shows the pots, pans and tools before you start.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Your list, recipes and photos stay on your device. New recipes download from our server, which receives nothing about you beyond the request itself.

Is it made for autistic people or people with disabilities?

It's made for everyone, with pictures, read-aloud and Simple mode designed so that people who shop by picture, children and learners can use it independently. It isn't a medical or therapy product.

Are the recipes healthy?

They're quick, vegetable-forward and labelled honestly: vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, high in protein or fibre. They're not medical or nutrition advice.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The starter recipes, your list and your recipes work without a connection; new recipes arrive when you're back online.
