A magnifier that translates

Point at a menu, a label, a sign or a letter.

  • Auto zoom sizes the text for you; pinch or double-tap whenever you like. The light is one tap away.
  • Translate paints the translation over the original, block by block, at about the size it's printed. Tap a block to hear both.
  • Reading colours: high contrast, inverted, yellow on black, white on blue.
  • Capture holds the picture, so you can lower the phone and keep reading, zoomed in further.
  • Reads vertical Japanese and Chinese too, on iOS 26 or later.

Read aloud, in your language

Put in your earphones and tap Read.

  • Nova View waits for a clear shot, holds it still on screen, and reads it top to bottom, translated into your language, with a large caption.
  • When clear text is found, a Text found card lets you save it or keep scanning.
  • Medicine codes show their expiry and batch, the small print that's hardest to read, and warn you when the date has passed.

Every QR code, checked first

No mode to switch on. When a code comes into view, the picture holds and you see where it really goes, in large type, before anything opens.

Likely a scam. Lookalike names like paypal-parking.com, letters from another alphabet, hidden scripts, app installs. Opening needs a second “Open anyway”.

Be careful. Short links that hide where they go, payment and sign-in pages, open Wi-Fi, premium-rate numbers.

No warning signs, and when a link is a well-known organisation's own site, whose it is.

  • The check runs on your device and never visits the link. Check where it goes follows the redirects only when you ask.
  • Product barcodes stay out of the way; aim at one to read, search or save it.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

No. Reading, scam checks and reading aloud work offline. Translation uses Apple's on-device translation; iOS downloads a language the first time you use it.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Camera frames, text and codes stay on your device, and Saved items stay in the app's own storage. There's no account and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

Can it promise a link is safe?

No checker can. Nova View catches the tricks scams use and explains them in plain words, but a link with no warning signs can still be dishonest. If a code asks you to pay or sign in, use the official app or website instead.

Is it a medical device?

No. It's a magnifier and reader for everyday print. Always follow the instructions from your pharmacist or doctor.
