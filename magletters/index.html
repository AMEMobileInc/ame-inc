MagLettersMagnetic letters for early learners

The fridge door, for first words.

MagLetters turns your iPad, iPhone or Mac into a magnetic-letter board. Children drag bright letters and toy stickers onto the board, build words and names, and hear every letter as they go, in English, Korean, Japanese, French or Spanish.

Download on the App Store ↗

Hands-on letters, at a glance

Tap a letter key and a magnet drops onto the board. Drag it anywhere, line it up with others, and a word appears. Nothing to read first and nothing to win: just letters to play with.

Build words by touch

Letters, numbers and symbols behave like real fridge magnets: they tumble in, slide where little fingers push them, and stay put.

  • Hear every letter. Recorded voices for English and Korean, and the device's own voices for Japanese, French and Spanish.
  • Stickers that talk. Drop a sticker next to a word and it says its name, so CAT can sit beside a cat.
  • A friendly phrase. The banner above the keys shows a short phrase with its English meaning, like 좋은 아침이에요! · Good morning! Turn Words off in Settings if you'd rather not.
  • Tidy up gently. Triple-tap a magnet to remove it, or tap the bin to clear the board, after a "Are you sure?".

Five languages, five boards

Switch language in Settings and the keys, the sounds, the stickers and the background all change with it. Great for bilingual families and for classrooms that welcome many languages.

Make the board their own

A fridge door, a blue sky, a calm colour, or a photo from your own library: the letters stick to whatever you choose.

  • Pictures: bright themed backgrounds made for each language.
  • Colour: any plain colour, for a calm board with nothing else on it.
  • My Photos: a family photo or a drawing. The photo stays on the device.
  • Share: save or send a finished creation through the share sheet.

Made with grown-ups in mind

Everything a child can't undo is one step away from small fingers.

  • A question for grown-ups before Contact, the Privacy Policy and Share.
  • No ads, no links to buy anything, no account and no in-app purchases.
  • Works offline. MagLetters doesn't use the internet at all.
  • Sound on or off, and the phrase banner on or off, in Settings.

iPad, iPhone and Mac

Best on iPad in landscape, where there's room for long words. On iPhone it fits a quick spelling game in the car. On Mac, drag letters with a mouse or trackpad; Command-Comma opens Settings and Command-K clears the board.

Questions

What age is MagLetters for?

It's rated 4+ and made for children of about three to seven, but any age that likes moving letters around is welcome.

Do I pay again for iPhone or Mac?

No. One purchase covers iPad, iPhone and Mac with the same Apple Account. On Mac, find it in the Mac App Store under your purchases.

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

Neither. Everything is included, and there's nothing to buy inside the app.

Does it need the internet?

No. Letters, sounds, stickers and backgrounds are all in the app, and it works offline.

Can my child use a photo from our library?

Yes, under Settings, then My Photos. iOS shows its own photo picker, so MagLetters sees only the photo you choose, and it stays on your device.
