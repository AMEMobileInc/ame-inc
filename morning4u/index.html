No pressure, ever

Morning4U is something to enjoy, not another thing to keep up with.

  • No streaks, scores or badges. Nothing to lose.
  • Not today? Pip rests under the stars, and that's perfectly okay.
  • Away for a while? Pip just says "Welcome back!" It never counts the days or looks sad.
  • A gentle reminder, only if you'd like one. One time for weekdays, a later one (or none) for weekends, and holidays off or like a weekend. Already opened Morning4U today? No reminder.

Getting started

  1. Meet Pip

    Tap Begin, or Set up my mornings to choose the kind of surprises you like: Surprise Me, Gentle, Curious or Playful.

  2. Choose a reminder, or don't

    Pick a weekday time, then in Settings choose a slower weekend time and your holidays (Canada, US or UK). Notifications are asked for only if you turn it on.

  3. Open it with your coffee

    Read today's surprise, then go about your day with it in mind.

  4. Keep what you find

    Tap Found something! to save a note, a photo or a feeling, whenever you like.

Questions

I used the old Morning4U. What changed?

Morning4U 2.0 is rebuilt from scratch. Your daily number, color and direction are still here, now as playful prompts. The news feeds and ads are gone, and Pip and the felt worlds are new.

What if I miss a day, or a week?

Nothing happens. There are no streaks to break. Pip welcomes you back and today's surprise is waiting.

Is the number, color and direction a fortune?

No. They're playful prompts to help you notice things, like spotting three of something or looking toward the west. You can hide them in Settings.

Does Morning4U need an internet connection?

No. Every discovery is built into the app, and everything works offline.

Is there an account, or ads?

Neither. There's no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking. Your moments and photos stay on your device.

Why didn't a reminder arrive?

If you'd already opened Morning4U that day, or it's a weekend or holiday you set to No reminder, that's on purpose. Otherwise check that Morning reminder is on in Morning4U's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for Morning4U in the iPhone Settings app. Reminders are planned for the next two months each time you open the app.
