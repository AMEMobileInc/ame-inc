Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how AME Mobile Inc. (“AME Mobile”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in our mobile applications and on amemobile.ca.

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Information we collect

Our apps are designed to work with as little personal information as possible. Depending on the app and the features you use, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as your email address when you contact us, or content you enter into an app.

Some apps may store preferences, settings, or app-created content on your device. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use personal information for advertising purposes.

Health and sensitive information

Our apps are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Unless an app clearly tells you otherwise, health-related information you enter remains on your device and is not transmitted to AME Mobile. Please review the in-app disclosures for the specific app you use.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our apps and website.
  • To respond to support requests and other messages you send us.
  • To diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, and protect the security of our services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Third-party services

Our apps may use services provided by Apple or other carefully selected providers for functions such as app distribution, subscriptions, payments, crash reporting, analytics, or optional AI features. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and only as needed to provide their services. Where an app uses a third-party service, the app’s own disclosures may provide additional details.

Data retention and deletion

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. You can delete locally stored app data by deleting the app or using its in-app controls, where available. To request deletion of information held by AME Mobile, contact us at info@amemobile.ca.

Visual Cue

Visual Cue keeps everything on your device: Visual Cues, photos, voice recordings, schedules, calendar plans, progress, and settings. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and it does not send this information to AME Mobile or anyone else. The camera, microphone, and photo library are used only when you choose to add a picture or record a voice, and reminders are local notifications created on your device. Importing data from an earlier version only reads the old data on the same device. Deleting the app deletes its data.

20.20.20: Eye Care & Tracker

20.20.20 has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Your break history, progress, and settings stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch and are not sent to AME Mobile.

From version 5.1, to keep its taps going with your wrist down, 20.20.20 runs each break on Apple Watch as a short Mind and Body workout. This needs your permission to write workouts to Apple Health. 20.20.20 doesn’t read anything from Apple Health, and each workout is discarded when the break ends, so nothing is saved there. No Health information leaves your devices or is sent to AME Mobile, and it is never used for advertising. You can turn this permission off at any time in the Health app, and breaks still work without it.

To deliver your reminders, the app sends our reminder service your device’s push token, your reminder schedule (days, hours, and interval), your sound and mute choices, and your time zone, with a random identifier created on your device. This information is not linked to your name or email address. It is used only to send your reminders, which reach your device through the Apple Push Notification service.

20.20.20 Plus is optional. Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through your Apple Account, and we do not receive your payment details or your name. The app checks your purchases with Apple on your device, including whether you bought the app before it became free, to unlock Plus. Your Plus status is shared only between your iPhone and Apple Watch and is not sent to our reminder service.

We remove this information once Apple tells us the app is no longer installed on the device. You can stop reminders at any time in the app, and you can ask us to delete this information by contacting us at info@amemobile.ca.

3000: Word Brain Challenge

3000 has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Your answers, scores, progress, and settings stay on your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch and are not sent to AME Mobile or anyone else. 3000 works without an internet connection.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. When you answer a reminder from the notification, the answer is recorded on your iPhone. The Apple Watch app sends your answers to your iPhone, and your iPhone sends back your score and streak, directly between your own devices. Watch face complications read this summary on the watch.

If you update from an earlier version, your earlier progress is read from the app’s own storage on your device and is not sent anywhere. 3000 Plus is optional. Purchases are handled by Apple through your Apple Account, and we do not receive your payment details or your name. Deleting the app deletes its data.

Actually: My Stories (formerly Message Actually)

Actually has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Your stories, the photos you add to them, your voice recordings, and the words you type stay on your iPhone or iPad and in its backups. They are not sent to AME Mobile, and videos are made on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to share or save a video.

Photos are chosen with Apple’s photo picker, so Actually sees only the photos you pick and keeps its own copy of them. Saving a video to your photo library uses add-only access. The microphone is used only while you are recording. When a photo has words but no recording, the words are read aloud by your device’s built-in voice, on the device.

If you used Message Actually, Actually brings your stories across from it on your device the first time it opens, without changing or removing Message Actually’s own files. Actually Plus is optional. Purchases are handled by Apple through your Apple Account, and we do not receive your payment details or your name. The app checks with Apple on your device whether you first downloaded Message Actually, so past users get Plus for life.

Deleting a story, or deleting Actually, removes its stories, photos, and recordings from the app. Photos and videos already saved in your photo library stay there. Settings, purchases, and links in the app are behind a question for grown-ups, because the app is made for children.

Amanda Chat

Amanda is an AI companion, not a replacement for professional care, and its reports are not a medical diagnosis. Unlike our on-device apps, Amanda works by sending what you share to our servers, so the health information statement above does not apply to it. What you tell Amanda may be sensitive, so please share only what you are comfortable with.

Your messages and your answers to Amanda’s questions are sent to our servers and to Microsoft’s Azure OpenAI service to generate Amanda’s replies and reports. Messages may also appear in our server logs. When Amanda creates a report, we store the report and its results with your account or a random identifier created on your device.

You can use Amanda without an account. If you create one, we store your email address and a hashed version of your password, or the email address provided by Sign in with Apple or Google. When you sign in on the Amanda website, we also record your IP address, browser details, and your approximate location, which we look up from your IP address using ipinfo.io. The website uses cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your consent choice and an anonymous identifier. If you use the Amanda contact form, we store your name, email address, and message.

Amanda Plus subscriptions are handled by Apple through your Apple Account, and we do not receive your payment details. We receive transaction details such as the product, purchase and expiry dates, and renewal status, and store them with your account or device identifier to unlock Plus.

To delete your Amanda account, reports, or other information we hold, contact us at info@amemobile.ca.

325 BC

325 BC is a thinking game played against our servers, so your answers are sent to AME Mobile. You can play without an account. The app creates a random identifier on your device, and we store your answers, whether they were correct, how long they took, and your campaign and Daily progress with that identifier. Medals and some Daily records are also kept on your device.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive and store an account identifier from Apple or Google, your email address, and your name if you choose to share it, and we link your play history to your account. Your name appears on public leaderboards only after you confirm a leaderboard name; until then you appear as “Player” followed by a short code. Your pixel avatar (the colours and items you choose) is stored with your account or the random identifier and shown next to your entry on leaderboards.

When you ask 325 BC to explain a wrong answer, the question and your wrong answer, without your name, email address, or identifier, are sent to Microsoft Azure AI services, which run Anthropic and OpenAI models, to write and check the explanation. We save explanations and reuse them for other players who give the same wrong answer.

325 BC+ is optional. Subscriptions are handled by Apple through your Apple Account, and we do not receive your payment details. We receive and store transaction details such as the product, purchase and expiry dates, and renewal status to unlock 325 BC+. Our servers run on Microsoft Azure, and their logs record IP addresses to keep the service secure. 325 BC has no advertising and no third-party analytics.

You can delete your 325 BC account at any time in the app under Settings → Account & avatar → Delete Account. This removes your account, leaderboard name, sign-in links, and the play history linked to it. Purchase records are kept, without your account, because Apple manages the subscription. If you play without an account, you can ask us to delete your history by contacting us at info@amemobile.ca.

Morning4U

Morning4U has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and it makes no network requests. Your daily cards, reminder settings, and saved moments, including any notes, feeling words, and photos you choose to keep, stay on your iPhone or iPad and are not sent to AME Mobile or anyone else.

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device, and notification permission is requested only if you turn the reminder on. Photos are chosen with Apple’s photo picker, so Morning4U sees only the photos you pick; it keeps its own copy in the app’s private storage and deletes it when you delete the moment. Prompts read aloud use your device’s built-in voice, on the device. You can delete all moments at any time in Settings, and deleting the app deletes its data. Morning4U does not share anything with other apps.

Children’s privacy

Our apps are not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.
