Destroyer DefenseRetro Naval Arcade Shooter

Defend your ship. Beat your high score.

You man the gun on the deck of a destroyer. Dive bombers, fighters, warships and submarines come in waves, and every wave is harder than the last. Drag to aim, hold to fire, and keep the ship afloat as long as you can, from the 1940s to the stars.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Free, with an optional one-time Plus. No account, no ads, no tracking. Plays offline.

Easy to pick up, one hand

Short battles in portrait, made for a few minutes at a time.

  1. Drag anywhere to aim

    The sight moves like a trackpad, so your thumb never covers what you're shooting at. Hold a finger down and the gun fires.

  2. Reload at the right moment

    When the ammo runs out, tap RELOAD, and tap again as the marker crosses the gold zone for a perfect reload and hot rounds. In later eras the gun heats up instead: VENT cools it.

  3. Answer every threat

    A periscope? Lob a depth charge. A torpedo on the way? Its track shows red across the water: shoot it. Chain kills for combos, catch crates for power-ups, and every wave you clear gives back a heart.

Three eras

The 1940s are free and complete. Plus adds two more, each with its own weapons, enemies, sounds and leaderboard, and each ends with its own credits when you clear wave 10.

A career at sea

Every run counts toward your service record.

  • Ranks: rise from Seaman to Admiral by how far you get.
  • Practice: three short drills, dive bombers, warships, and subs and torpedoes, that end once you've mastered them, with gold, silver and bronze medals.
  • Ribbons: 23 to earn, from a perfect reload streak to slaying the Leviathan.
  • The Fleet: your ship in each era, with its specs and your best wave.
  • Game Center: a leaderboard for each era.

Questions

Is it a realistic naval simulation?

No, it's a casual arcade game for fun. The ships, weapons and enemies are made up, and there are no real nations, flags or insignia.

Does it need an internet connection, or an account?

Neither. Everything plays offline. Game Center is optional and used only for the leaderboards, and a connection is used only to buy or restore Plus.

Does it work on iPad and iPhone Duo?

Yes. The game plays at the same speed on every device, and the buttons spread out on bigger screens.

I bought Plus on another device. How do I get it back?

Open Plus from the menu and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account.
